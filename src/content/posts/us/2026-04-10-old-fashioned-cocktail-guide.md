The Old Fashioned dates back to 1806. It calls for just four ingredients, but every one deserves attention.

## The Recipe

- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey
- 1 sugar cube and 2 dashes of Angostura bitters
- Expressed oils from an orange peel
- One large ice cube

## The Key

Don't add soda water or muddle fruit into it. That's not an Old Fashioned. That's fruit salad.

## Whiskey Picks

- **Beginner:** Buffalo Trace
- **Intermediate:** Knob Creek 12 Year
- **Splurge:** George T. Stagg
