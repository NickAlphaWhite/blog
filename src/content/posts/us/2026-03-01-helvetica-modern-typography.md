Created in 1957, Helvetica was still everywhere in 2026: airports, subway stations, and brand identities.

## Why?

- **Neutrality:** It doesn't impose a mood or compete with the message.
- **Legibility:** A generous x-height helps it stay clear at a distance.
- **Versatility:** It works on everything from road signs to luxury logos.

## The Modern Debate

Some people call it boring. But boring and neutral aren't the same thing. Boring means not making a choice; neutrality can be a deliberate choice.
