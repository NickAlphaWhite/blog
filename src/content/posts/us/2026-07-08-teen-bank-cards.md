Here are the bank cards I have or recommend for people ages 16–18, particularly if, like me, you need a way to pay while traveling abroad.

## ICBC

A regular debit card from one of the world's largest banks. My dad opened it for me using my household registration booklet, and it was my very first bank card. ICBC has branches everywhere, which is a big advantage.

## Bank of China (BOC)

A standard Visa multi-currency credit card. I needed a credit card to pay an international competition fee, so I had to get one. It's an additional card under my dad's account, with a generous credit limit. The downside is that he knows about every transaction I make.

## China Merchants Bank (CMB)

**The clear winner:** Golden Sunflower Mastercard, standard Mastercard debit card, and regular debit card.

CMB has been the most minor-friendly bank I've encountered. I've visited its branches near Wanda Plaza in Jiangning, Baijiahu, and the Jiangning branch. Staff at every location were polite and welcoming to young customers. When I went to Baijiahu to apply for a Mastercard debit card before it had been widely publicized, the teller hadn't heard much about it but still helped me open the account based on the information I provided.

I also like how CMB's Mastercard works: transactions settle in Chinese yuan without requiring me to exchange currency in advance, and the card has been reliably accepted. I used it smoothly in Hong Kong, and the authorization hold was only 100% of the purchase amount.

> **Highly recommended. If you're traveling overseas, this is the card I'd choose first.** If you're 16–18 and want your own bank account—or need a card for travel—CMB is my top pick.

## GOPAY

This prepaid Mastercard is associated with Spring Airlines. As far as I could find, it was the only non-mainland-issued Mastercard that minors could open and add to Apple Pay. Unfortunately, top-ups have temporarily been suspended for reasons outside the company's control, and I don't know when they'll return.
