Bangkok's streets are the world's best open-air kitchen. Three days, nine meals, and no backtracking.

## Day 1: Old Town

- **Breakfast:** Jok Prince rice porridge
- **Lunch:** Pad Thai at Thipsamai
- **Dinner:** Soi Rambuttri night market

## Day 2: Chinatown (Yaowarat Road)

- Yaowarat oyster omelets
- T&K Seafood
- Charcoal-grilled squid with spicy sauce

## Day 3: Sukhumvit

- Wattana Panich beef noodles (a 45-year-old institution)
- Mango sticky rice at Mae Varee
- Final stop: Gaggan Anand—from street food to the avant-garde

Three days of eating cost less than a single Michelin-starred meal.
