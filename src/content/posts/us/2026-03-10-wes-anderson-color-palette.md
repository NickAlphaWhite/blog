Wes Anderson's movies might be the best advertisements Pantone could ever ask for. Pink, pale yellow, mint green, and caramel brown—every symmetrical frame feels like a lesson in graphic design. My recommended trio: *The Grand Budapest Hotel* → *The French Dispatch* → *Asteroid City*.
