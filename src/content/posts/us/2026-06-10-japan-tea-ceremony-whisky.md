After Kyoto, I stopped at the Yamazaki Distillery, then reflected on the contrasting philosophies of Japanese drinks through the Omotesenke tea tradition.

## Yamazaki Distillery

Located between Osaka and Kyoto, the distillery offers visits by reservation. Highlights include:

- **Whisky museum:** More than 5,000 bottles of whisky and spirits.
- **Tasting:** A side-by-side look at Yamazaki 12, 18, and 25 Year.
- **Exclusive releases:** Limited single-cask bottlings sold on site.

## The Omotesenke Tea Ceremony

An experience of the tea ceremony at Fushin-an reveals the spirit of *wa, kei, sei, jaku*—harmony, respect, purity, and tranquility.

## Between the Two

Whisky seeks complexity; the tea ceremony embraces empty space. One moves outward, the other inward.
