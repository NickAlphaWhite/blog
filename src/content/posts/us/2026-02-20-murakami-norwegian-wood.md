Published in 1987 and reread in 2026, *Norwegian Wood* feels like a completely different book.

At twenty, you read it as a story about young love. At thirty, the line that stays with you is that death isn't the opposite of life, but a part of it. Watanabe, Naoko, and Midori are lonely in three different ways. It's Murakami's least Murakami-like novel—and perhaps his most painful.
