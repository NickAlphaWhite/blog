How can a minor open an account with no opening deposit? Here's what I learned at a CITIC Bank branch in Nanjing.

## About the Card

CITIC Bank's Black Pixiu Mastercard World Debit Card is an international debit card aimed at younger customers.

## Opening an Account

### What to Bring

1. A national ID card (or household registration booklet)
2. Student ID, if available
3. A parent or guardian, if required by the branch

### Steps

1. Find a CITIC Bank branch that issues the card.
2. Tell the teller you'd like to apply for the "Mastercard World Debit Card."
3. Complete the application form.
4. Wait for approval, usually one to two business days.

## My Experience

- Supports purchases abroad
- Convenient for online payments
- Beautiful card design

## Things to Know

> If you're under 18, call the branch first to confirm that it can open an account for you. It could save you a wasted trip.
