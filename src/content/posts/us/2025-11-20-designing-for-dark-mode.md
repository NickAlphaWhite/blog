Dark mode isn't as simple as replacing a white background with a black one.

## Why Dark Mode Can Feel More Tiring

When you read light-colored text against a dark background, your pupils dilate and your depth of field becomes shallower. That's one reason text shouldn't be too thin or glaringly white.

## Core Principles

1. **Avoid pure black:** `#000` can be harsh. Try `#0a0a0c`.
2. **Avoid pure white text:** `#f5f5f7` is more comfortable than `#fff`.
3. **Increase font weight slightly:** With SF Pro, Medium may work better than Regular in dark mode.
4. **Replace shadows with borders:** `box-shadow` can nearly disappear on a dark surface; use a `border` or subtle glow instead.

## Color System

Reduce saturation by roughly 10–15%. The same hue can look more saturated against a dark background.

## Takeaway

The challenge with dark mode isn't color. It's light.
