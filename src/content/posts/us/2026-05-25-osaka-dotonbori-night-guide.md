Dotonbori is Osaka's stomach. Walk the kilometer from Shinsaibashi to Namba and you'll never run out of food to try.

Don't miss takoyaki at Takoyaki Juhachiban, okonomiyaki at Chibo, and kushikatsu at Daruma. The neon signs above the Dotonbori Canal may be even more famous than the food. Getting a photo with the Glico Running Man sign is practically a city ritual.
