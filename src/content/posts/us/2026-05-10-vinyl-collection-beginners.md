## Your First Records

1. Miles Davis — *Kind of Blue*
2. Pink Floyd — *The Dark Side of the Moon*
3. Ryuichi Sakamoto — *BTTB*
4. Nujabes — *Modal Soul*

## A Word of Advice

Don't buy too many. The magic of vinyl is in **listening to one record**, not in **owning everything**.
