Staz Hotel Premier Dongtan is in the new town of Dongtan, Hwaseong, in Gyeonggi Province. After staying at several business hotels in South Korea, I found this one a pleasant surprise—cleaner than I expected and more comfortable than I'd imagined. The only drawback is its distance from central Seoul. But if most of your plans are in Dongtan, it's an excellent choice.

---

## Location

Dongtan is a relatively new urban district in southern Gyeonggi Province, about an hour's drive from Seoul. The Times Terrace shopping center is right next to the hotel, and a large Homeplus supermarket is within walking distance. There are plenty of restaurants and cafés nearby, so finding something to eat is easy.

Airport bus 8837 has a stop about a six-minute walk away. There are also lots of local buses. But if your main goal is sightseeing in Seoul, think carefully about spending roughly two hours a day commuting.

---

## Rooms

The rooms have a typical Korean business-hotel layout: compact but well planned. The memory-foam mattress is on the soft side, unlike the hard beds in many Korean hotels, which I appreciated. The down comforter is comfortable, too. The bathroom has an electronic bidet seat (비데), practically a standard feature in Korea.

The air-conditioning is centrally controlled, so you can't set the temperature yourself. Cooling may even be turned off during the day. If you're staying in summer and get hot easily, that's worth knowing. You can open the window, but you'll hear traffic outside.

Rooms facing the road can be noisy. If you're a light sleeper, ask for a room on the quieter side of the building when you check in. There's also a convenience store on the ground floor—handy for buying drinks and snacks.

---

## Facilities and Service

The gym has fairly new equipment and long operating hours, which should appeal to business travelers.

Breakfast is served at Garden 171. The selection is limited, and the menu gets repetitive if you're staying for several days, but the food is decent. Front-desk service varies. Some staff members I encountered were genuinely warm and helpful, while others were rather indifferent.

The laundry room is reserved for corporate clients with special agreements; regular guests can't use it. There's a coin laundromat across the street, but making that walk in winter isn't much fun.

---

## Verdict

Staz Hotel Premier Dongtan is a straightforward Korean business hotel. It knows exactly who it's for: people visiting Dongtan on business or working near Samsung Electronics who need a clean, comfortable place to stay.

If you're spending a few days in this part of Gyeonggi, it's a great option. If you're on vacation and plan to spend most of your time in Seoul, it's simply too far away.
