I spent three days wandering around Kyoto with a Leica M6 and a roll of Portra 400.

## Favorite Places to Shoot

1. **Fushimi Inari:** The thousands of torii gates are worth getting up early for, when the light is right.
2. **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove:** Vertical compositions work better than horizontal ones.
3. **Hanamikoji Street:** The best light lasts about 20 minutes at dusk.
4. **Pontocho:** Lanterns and stone-paved streets after dark.
5. **Nanzenji Aqueduct:** Red-brick arches, less obvious than other locations but very photogenic.

## Film vs. Digital

Portra 400 renders skin tones and warmth in a way I can't quite reproduce in Lightroom. I also wasted six frames by missing focus.

## What I Learned

The best photos aren't always planned. A sunset shot I took while lost in Narai-juku ended up being the best frame on the roll.
