We're halfway through 2026, and a few R&B albums have earned a permanent spot in my rotation.

## My Top Five

1. **SZA — LANA (Deluxe):** More of the *SOS* universe, but more personal and more fractured.
2. **Frank Ocean — (He Finally Released It):** Eight years of waiting, and worth it.
3. **Daniel Caesar — Never Enough II**
4. **FKA twigs — EUSEXUA:** So experimental that it barely feels like R&B.
5. **Sampha — Satellite Business**

## Trends

- Auto-Tune is fading; natural voices are coming back.
- Afrobeats continue to shape the genre.
- Albums are getting shorter, often around 30–40 minutes.

## Weekend Playlist

The new Frank Ocean, SZA, and Sampha, on repeat on a Saturday morning.
