The Pier First Class Lounge near Gate 65 at Hong Kong International Airport was designed by Ilse Crawford and is often described as Asia's finest airline lounge.

## The Space

The lounge is divided into a tea room, bar, dining area, showers, and relaxation spaces. Warm, subdued colors and generous use of copper, leather, and wood create a calm atmosphere.

## Dining

- **À la carte restaurant:** Try the dan dan noodles and char siu rice.
- **Bar:** The champagne bar serves Moët & Chandon.
- **Tea room:** Traditional gongfu tea prepared by a dedicated attendant.

## Showers

Eight private shower suites with Aesop amenities and no time limit.

## My Take

More comfortable than many hotel lobbies. A four-hour layover somehow didn't feel long enough.
