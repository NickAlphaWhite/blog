In September, I learned that I could start a Marriott Platinum Challenge for just RMB 188, so I signed up without hesitation. The challenge could begin as late as December 31, which made the whole thing seem manageable.

Still, I had to stay eight nights, and finishing before April 2026 would take some effort. My winter break was probably going to turn into a hotel-hopping marathon. Luckily, studying for the SAT gave me one more excuse to squeeze in a few hotel nights.

The most important part was completing the final night in February. That way, I could enjoy a full calendar year of status.

> Wish me luck. I'll post updates here as soon as I have them.

---

## How It Ended

I didn't finish. In the end, I managed just one night, nowhere near the eight-night goal.

Still, RMB 188 wasn't too much to spend on the dream.
