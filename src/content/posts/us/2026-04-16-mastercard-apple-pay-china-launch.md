At 7:30 this morning, Apple Pay support for mainland China-issued Mastercards officially went live. The first four participating banks are Agricultural Bank of China, Bank of China, CITIC Bank, and Shanghai Pudong Development Bank.

Visa launched with eight banks in January, making Mastercard the second international card network to join Apple Pay in China. After ten years of waiting, both networks have arrived within three months.

## Background: Mastercard NUCC Paved the Way

Apple Pay launched in China in February 2016, but for nearly a decade it supported UnionPay only. Standalone Visa and Mastercard cards couldn't be added.

The turning point came in November 2023, when the People's Bank of China approved Mastercard NUCC's bank-card clearing license. The joint venture officially began operating in May 2024, paving the way for yuan-denominated Mastercards and, eventually, Apple Pay.

Visa got there first this January, with credit cards from eight banks and CITIC's debit card. Now Mastercard has followed.

## How to Add Your Card

There are two methods. I recommend using your bank's app.

**Method 1: Add it in Wallet**

Open Wallet on your iPhone → tap the plus sign in the upper-right corner → choose "Debit or Credit Card" → select your bank → follow the verification steps.

Note: Your iPhone's region must be set to mainland China. If you've switched regions before, the Wallet method may fail. In that case, go straight to Method 2.

**Method 2: Add it through your bank's app (recommended)**

Search for "Apple Pay" or "Mobile Pay" in your banking app. You'll be sent to Wallet from the setup page, regardless of your iPhone's region setting.

- **Agricultural Bank of China:** Home → All → Mobile Pay → follow the setup instructions
- **Bank of China, CITIC, and SPD Bank:** Search for "Apple Pay" at the top of the app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Common issues on launch day:

- **Nothing happens in Wallet:** Your iPhone region may not be mainland China. Try the banking app.
- **The card setup screen is missing:** Update iOS.
- **Activation fails:** Your card may be locked. Check its status in the banking app.
- **Can I add a debit card?** CITIC Mastercard debit cards work; Bank of China's Mastercard debit cards don't yet. If you don't have a credit card, CITIC is currently your only choice.
- **Can I pay in mainland China?** No. Mastercard on Apple Pay currently works for contactless payments abroad, not domestic UnionPay terminals.
- **Why bother?** You can travel without carrying the physical card, benefit from tokenized transactions, and qualify for card-linking promotions.

## A Quick Comparison With Visa

Visa launched in January with eight banks, including ICBC, Bank of Communications, China Merchants Bank, Ping An Bank, and Industrial Bank. Mastercard has only four launch partners, a noticeably smaller rollout.

But Mastercard has one advantage worth watching: CITIC's Mastercard debit card works with Apple Pay from day one. Visa debit support is also limited to CITIC for now. However, China Merchants Bank has already issued a Mastercard debit card; if it gets Apple Pay support, that could be especially useful for younger cardholders.

Mastercard also sometimes offers a slight exchange-rate advantage on currencies such as the Japanese yen or Singapore dollar. Small differences can add up.

## What's Still Missing?

Four banks are a good start, but ICBC, China Construction Bank, Bank of Communications, China Merchants Bank, and Ping An Bank aren't on board yet. Visa already covers most major banks. Mastercard has some catching up to do.

Domestic acceptance is still missing, too. If you want to use Mastercard through Apple Pay at a convenience store in China, you'll have to wait for Mastercard NUCC's domestic point-of-sale acceptance network.

At least, after a decade, iPhone users in China finally have more than UnionPay and Visa in their Wallet.
