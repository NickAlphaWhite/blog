Before his death in 2023, "the Professor" left a playlist in *GQ* while he was ill.

## The Tracks

1. Debussy — *Clair de Lune*
2. Erik Satie — *Gymnopédie No. 1*
3. John Cage — *4'33"*
4. David Bowie — "Heroes"
5. Glenn Gould — *Goldberg Variations* (1981)

## Why These Pieces?

Cage's *4'33"* is about silence. For someone facing the final stages of illness, silence can become more precious than music.

## His Legacy

Sakamoto didn't just leave notes on a page. He left us a way of listening. Even from his sickbed, he continued collecting the sound of rain and shaping it into *12*.
