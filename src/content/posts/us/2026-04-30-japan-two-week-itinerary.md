Two weeks, four destinations, and no backtracking across Japan.

## Days 1–4: Tokyo

- Shinjuku, Shibuya, and Omotesando
- teamLab Borderless in Odaiba
- Breakfast at Tsukiji Outer Market and the Ginza skyline at night
- **Where to stay:** The Ginza area

## Days 5–8: Kyoto

- Fushimi Inari → Kiyomizu-dera → Ninenzaka
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and Tenryu-ji
- Nishiki Market and dinner in Pontocho
- **Optional:** Kifune Shrine and Kurama Onsen

## Days 9–10: Naoshima

- Chichu Art Museum (Tadao Ando)
- Benesse House
- Yayoi Kusama's pumpkins

## Days 11–13: Osaka

- Dotonbori and Shinsaibashi
- Osaka Castle
- Tsutenkaku and Shinsekai

## Budget

About RMB 18,000 per person, including flights and accommodations—much better value than a comparable trip to Europe.
