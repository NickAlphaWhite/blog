Burgundy's 2020 vintage was hot and dry, producing concentrated fruit and ripe tannins.

## Tasting Notes

| Producer | Vineyard / Appellation | Score |
|----------|-------------------------|-------|
| Domaine Leroy | Vosne-Romanée 1er Cru | 97 |
| DRC | Richebourg | 99 |
| Méo-Camuzet | Cros Parantoux | 96 |
| Sylvain Cathiard | Malconsorts | 95 |

## Takeaway

2020 is a vintage you can drink now or cellar for another 20 years. Leroy and DRC are unforgettable.
