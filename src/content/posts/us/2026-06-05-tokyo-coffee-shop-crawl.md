Tokyo has one of the world's highest concentrations of coffee shops.

## The Eight-Stop List

1. **Koffee Mameya:** The focus is on beans, not cups.
2. **Fuglen:** A little Norway in Yoyogi.
3. **Onibus:** The soul of Nakameguro's neighborhood coffee scene.
4. **Glitch:** A master of light roasts.
5. **Café de l'Ambre:** A Ginza legend, founded in 1948.
6. **Switch Coffee:** A tiny six-square-meter shop in Meguro.
7. **Bongen:** Exceptional lattes in Ginza 2-chome.
8. **Sidewalk Stand:** The smallest storefront, the biggest surprise.

## Final Thought

In Tokyo, every cup of coffee is taken seriously.
