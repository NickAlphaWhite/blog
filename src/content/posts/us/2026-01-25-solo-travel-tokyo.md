Tokyo is one of the best cities for solo travel. It's safe, convenient, and never boring.

## Why Go to Tokyo Alone?

- Seats for one are everywhere: ramen shops, izakayas, and sushi counters.
- Capsule hotels and 9h hotels are practically made for solo travelers.
- You don't have to compromise with anyone. Want to visit Tsukiji at 4 a.m.? Just go.

## Five-Day Route

**Day 1:** Shinjuku — late-night food in Omoide Yokocho  
**Day 2:** Kichijoji — Inokashira Park and used bookstores  
**Day 3:** Kiyosumi-Shirakawa — Blue Bottle Coffee and Kiyosumi Gardens  
**Day 4:** Yanesen — Yanaka Ginza and Nezu Shrine  
**Day 5:** Shimokitazawa — vintage clothes, live music venues, and curry

## Budget

Five days, including airfare, on RMB 3,500 can be very comfortable.
