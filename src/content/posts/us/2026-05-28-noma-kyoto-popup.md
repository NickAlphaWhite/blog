René Redzepi and the Noma team opened a three-month pop-up restaurant near Tofuku-ji Temple in Kyoto.

## Background

After closing its Copenhagen restaurant, Noma made Kyoto the first stop on its worldwide tour. The pop-up took over a renovated, century-old machiya townhouse.

## The 12-Course Menu

- Sakura shrimp with fermented rice
- Kyoto duck breast with wild mushroom jus
- Matcha and yuzu dessert
- Fermentation as a recurring thread throughout the meal

## Atmosphere

An open kitchen, with Redzepi personally serving and explaining dishes. Just 14 guests were seated that evening.

## Price

¥68,000 per person, plus ¥35,000 for wine pairings. Worth every penny.
