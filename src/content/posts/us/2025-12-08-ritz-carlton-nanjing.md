On December 17, I visited the lounge and front desk at The Ritz-Carlton, Nanjing, and managed to get two room key cards. I wouldn't call the hotel's design breathtaking, but it's certainly not unattractive. The typography feels right for a luxury hotel. Better yet, each key card carries a local place name—a nice touch for anyone who likes collecting them.

![The Ritz-Carlton NJ](https://img.remit.ee/api/file/BQACAgUAAyEGAASHRsPbAAEOBjNpSnqCWGxQky74iBwc43-XW0wW1AACdiQAAig5UFYrkGpXj5TQRzYE.jpg)

## Food and Drinks

The coffee and cake were both delicious. Prices were a little steep, but perfectly reasonable for a Ritz-Carlton. They also offer coffee to go; it tastes great and is surprisingly inexpensive.

## Lounge Atmosphere

The lounge has a lovely, quiet atmosphere, although guests occasionally walk through and talk. I'm not sure what fragrance the hotel uses, but I found it a little too strong—not quite what I expected from the place.
