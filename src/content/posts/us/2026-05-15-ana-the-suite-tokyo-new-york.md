A trip report on ANA's The Suite first class, flying from Tokyo Haneda to New York JFK in its newly designed private cabin.

## Ground Experience

Haneda has a dedicated ANA Suite Check-in area and a separate security checkpoint. The Suite Lounge offers made-to-order meals; I recommend the sushi and Japanese curry.

## The Cabin

The Boeing 777-300ER has just eight first-class seats, each with a 43-inch 4K screen and a sliding door that closes completely.

## Dining

- Caviar with champagne (Salon 2007)
- Five-course kaiseki
- Teppanyaki-style wagyu beef

## Verdict

One of my best first-class experiences so far. Exceptional privacy and impeccable service.
