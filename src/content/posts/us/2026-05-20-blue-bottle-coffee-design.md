Blue Bottle isn't just selling coffee. It's selling the art of subtraction.

## Visual Identity

- **Logo:** A blue bottle silhouette, without any words
- **Colors:** White, blue, and wood tones. Nothing else.
- **Space:** The original Kiyosumi-Shirakawa location, designed by Schemata, has a minimalist warehouse aesthetic.

## Packaging

No unnecessary labels, no glossy finish, no extra words. Just a handwritten roast date on the coffee bag.

## The Lesson

Good design isn't about how much you add. It's about removing things until nothing else needs to go. Blue Bottle proves the point.
