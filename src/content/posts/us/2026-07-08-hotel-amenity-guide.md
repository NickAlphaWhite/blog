Hotel bathroom amenities are an often-overlooked detail. For me, the brand I see when I open the bathroom door matters almost as much as getting a room upgrade.

## Four Seasons

- **Frédéric Malle** — Eau de Magnolia
- **Acqua di Parma**
- **Miller Harris**
- **L'Occitane**

## Mandarin Oriental

- **Diptyque** — The Parisian fragrance house has become a classic Mandarin Oriental partner.
- **Bottega Veneta** — A crossover from the luxury leather-goods brand.

## Marriott Bonvoy

### BVLGARI

Naturally, BVLGARI hotels stock a full lineup of BVLGARI toiletries. The White Tea collection is a signature.

### EDITION

**LE LABO** custom fragrance, blended specifically for EDITION and not sold separately.

### Marriott

**thisworks** — A British natural-skincare brand known for its sleep-focused products.

## IHG

### InterContinental

**Byredo BLANCHE** — A clean scent from the Swedish fragrance house, with white rose and pink pepper.

## Hyatt

### Park Hyatt

- **LE LABO BERGAMOTE 22** — Bergamot-forward, citrus and aromatic notes
- **LE LABO SANTAL 33** — Sandalwood and leather, a Park Hyatt favorite
- **Aesop** — Australian botanical skincare
