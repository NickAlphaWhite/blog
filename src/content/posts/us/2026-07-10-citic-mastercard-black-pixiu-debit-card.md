### February 27, 2026: Opening a Black Pixiu Card With No Initial Deposit at a CITIC Branch in Nanjing

### Even though this is a Mastercard debit card, it's still a World Mastercard, so in theory there are asset requirements. At the strictest branches, you might need RMB 50,000 on deposit at CITIC. At others, transferring in tens of thousands of yuan is enough; some will even accept proof that you have RMB 500,000 at another bank.

### But I'm a high school student with no job. There is no way I could show half a million yuan in assets. After countless phone calls and online searches, I finally found a branch willing to issue one without a deposit. I won't name the branch here, to avoid bothering its staff.

### Because I'm still a minor, though, a guardian had to apply on my behalf. It was a hassle, but fortunately my mom was understanding and generous enough to come along.

### The process involved facial verification for my guardian, photos of our household registration booklet, and entering information from both of our ID cards. It took a little time, but the teller clearly knew what she was doing, and we finished in about ten minutes.

### Afterward, I wanted to deposit four yuan in banknotes I had found at home, but the staff asked for the household registration booklet again. I was stunned by how cumbersome this was.

### At least online transfers work, so the card isn't just a decoration. The teller said online banking wasn't available, but I tested it and found that some online-banking functions still worked. I haven't tested whether transfers are supported through that channel.

### The card has accounts in eight currencies, so you can exchange money in advance and hold balances in those currencies. Although automatic currency exchange has been introduced, I've heard transactions may still go through U.S. dollars and incur an extra conversion cost. Others say the eight common currencies convert directly using CITIC's rates. I haven't figured out exactly which explanation is correct. For now, China Merchants Bank's Mastercard, with Mastercard's exchange rate, is still the easiest option for me.

### Overseas payments on this card trigger an authorization hold of 105% of the transaction amount.

### Next on my list are Bank of China's Monet card and China Guangfa Bank's Black Koi card. I'd also like CITIC's China Southern Airlines co-branded card if I can find a branch that doesn't require proof of assets. That may be a lot harder.
