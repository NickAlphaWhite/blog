A look at Singapore Airlines' new A380 Suites, flying from Shanghai Pudong to Singapore Changi.

## Check-In

Singapore Airlines has a dedicated check-in area in Pudong's Terminal 2. First-class passengers receive concierge assistance through departure formalities and all the way to the lounge.

## The Cabin

Six suites occupy the front of the upper deck. Each has a separate seat and a bench that can be made into a double bed. The cabin is upholstered in Poltrona Frau leather.

## In-Flight Service

- **Dining:** Preorder lobster thermidor through Book the Cook.
- **Wine list:** Krug Grande Cuvée and a selection of Burgundies.
- **Comforts:** Lalique pajamas and Givenchy amenities.

## Arriving at Changi

Private car transfer to The Private Room—the best first-class lounge in the world, bar none.
