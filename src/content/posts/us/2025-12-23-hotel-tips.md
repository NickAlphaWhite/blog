Book → get your confirmation number → call a manager → explain what you're hoping for. Here's a step-by-step approach to getting the best possible treatment.

## Step 1: Make the Reservation

Book through the hotel's official website or app and save your confirmation number. You'll need it for every conversation that follows.

## Step 2: Call the Hotel

Rather than emailing, try calling directly. Ask to speak with the front office manager or guest relations manager.

## Step 3: Explain Your Request

- Be polite and clear about what you're looking for.
- Mention a special occasion, such as a birthday or anniversary, if you're celebrating one.
- Ask whether an upgrade might be possible.

### Sample Phrases

1. "Hi, I have a reservation for [date], confirmation number [XXX]. Would a suite upgrade be possible?"
2. "I'd really appreciate it if there were any special arrangements you could make."

## A Few Reminders

- Don't demand anything. Stay courteous.
- The earlier you reach out, the better your chances.
- Hotel loyalty status can help.

> The keys are politeness, clarity, and planning ahead.
