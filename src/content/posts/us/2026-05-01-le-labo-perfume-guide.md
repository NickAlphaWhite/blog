Each Le Labo City Exclusive fragrance is usually sold only in its namesake city, with a worldwide release window every September.

## My Collection So Far

- **Santal 33** (New York): Leather and sandalwood, the classic everyone seems to own
- **Thé Noir 29** (general collection): Black tea and fig
- **Gaiac 10** (Tokyo): Guaiac wood, minimal and tranquil
- **Vanille 44** (Paris): Bourbon vanilla, subtle and elegant
- **Baie Rose 26** (Chicago): Pink pepper and clove

## What's Next?

My target: Citron 28 (Seoul)—a perfect balance of citrus and jasmine.

## Buying Tips

City Exclusives start at 50 mL and cost about 30% more than the regular line. But if you really love one, why wait a year?
